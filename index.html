THE ART OF UNMAKING

A corpse is a library. Read it with your fingertips.

HOLD + DRAG over the corpse. Each pixel first glows bright violet while still attached, then detaches as a deep-purple fragment pulled toward the hand.

Only the topmost material can be removed. Reveal flesh → cloth → dried remains → skeleton. The green background is never destroyed.

Pan using the bottom arrows (or keyboard arrow keys). Beware pulsing corruption hidden beneath the flesh.

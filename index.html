THE ART OF UNMAKING

A corpse is a library. Read it with your fingertips.

HOLD + DRAG over the corpse. Pixels first glow bright purple while attached, then detach as deep purple particles pulled toward your hand.

Only locally uncovered layers can be harvested. Strip away most of the flesh surrounding an area before touching the layer beneath. Cloth conceals dried remains only where the cloth exists.

Sever the connection to a patch of tissue and it falls as one connected piece, landing on the ground defined by the skeleton's lowest pixels. Harvest dropped pieces with your hand.

Bones are the final layer. Every last pixel of flesh, cloth, and dried remains must reach your hand before the skeleton can be harvested.

Pan with the left / right arrows or your keyboard. Beware pulsing patches of corruption.
